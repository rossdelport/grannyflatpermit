City of Armadale granny flat rules

Short answer

In the City of Armadale you can build one granny flat up to 70 m² per property. A building permit is always needed, and work worth $20,000 or more needs a registered builder or owner-builder approval. The City decides in 10 business days (BA1) or 25 (BA2).

Last updated 25 November 2026

The main rules

  • Size: up to 70 m², not counting open verandahs or carports.
  • How many: one granny flat per property.
  • Type: detached, attached, or built into the house.
  • Permit: a building permit is always needed before work starts. Health or planning approval may be needed first.

Who can build it

If the work is worth $20,000 or more, you need a registered builder or owner-builder approval. A registered builder must also get a home indemnity insurance certificate for a granny flat over $20,000. Owner-builders don't need one at this stage.

Timeframes

  • BA1 (certified by a private building surveyor): up to 10 business days.
  • BA2 (checked by the City): up to 25 business days.
  • The permit lasts 2 years, and the builder sends a BA7 within 7 days of finishing.

Questions

Common questions

How big can a granny flat be in the City of Armadale?

Up to 70 m², not counting open verandahs or carports. Only one is allowed per property.

Do I need a building permit for a granny flat in Armadale?

Yes, always, before any work starts. Health or planning approval may also be needed first.

Do I need a registered builder for a granny flat in Armadale?

If the work is worth $20,000 or more, you need a registered builder or owner-builder approval.

What does Armadale want on a granny flat site plan?

The granny flat, other buildings, distances to boundaries, septic tanks if any, a 2 m x 2 m tree planting area, and landscaping and paving in the front setback.
