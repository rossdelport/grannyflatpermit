Town of Bassendean granny flat rules

Short answer

In the Town of Bassendean, every new granny flat needs a building permit before work starts. Planning approval usually isn't needed if it's up to 70 m² and meets the R-Codes. The Town decides certified applications within 10 business days and uncertified ones within 25.

Last updated 28 November 2026

Building permit: always

The Town of Bassendean says anyone building a new granny flat (ancillary accommodation), or adding to or altering one, needs a building permit before work starts. That includes changes to the floor, walls or roof, and turning a room from habitable to non-habitable (or the other way), or a wet area into a dry one. See granny flat building permits.

Non-structural fit-out, such as tiling, cabinets, new light or tap fittings, rendering and painting, doesn't need a permit. It still has to meet the building standards.

Planning approval

Under WA's planning rules, a granny flat of up to 70 m² that meets the R-Codes deemed-to-comply rules usually doesn't need planning approval. The Town says other approvals, such as planning, septic or Heritage Council approval, may be needed before the building permit, so check these first. See granny flats without planning approval.

BA1 or BA2?

FormWho issues the design certificateTown's time limit
BA1 (certified)An independent building surveying contractor10 business days
BA2 (uncertified)The Town25 business days

The Town says most applications get a response sooner.

What to send

  • The completed application form and fees.
  • An owner-builder certificate, or the builder's home indemnity insurance certificate, if the work is over $20,000.
  • Plans, such as site, floor, elevation and section drawings, plus structural details, energy efficiency details, termite treatment and a BAL assessment if needed.
  • Neighbour consent forms (BA20 or BA20A) if you'll remove a shared fence, go onto their land, encroach, or dig near the boundary.
  • A Construction Training Fund levy receipt, if your job needs one.

The Town prefers applications by email to mail@bassendean.wa.gov.au. If the job needs a new crossover, use of the verge or a drainage connection, lodge the Town's infrastructure permit form too. Check your size with the granny flat size calculator.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a building permit for a granny flat in Bassendean?

Yes. The Town of Bassendean says a building permit is needed before building or altering a granny flat.

Do I need planning approval for a granny flat in Bassendean?

Usually not if it's up to 70 m² and meets the R-Codes deemed-to-comply rules, but heritage, septic or other approvals may be needed first.

How long does Bassendean take to issue a building permit?

Up to 10 business days for a certified (BA1) application and 25 for an uncertified (BA2) one. The Town says most are faster.

When do I need my neighbour's consent in Bassendean?

If the work removes a shared fence, needs access to their land, encroaches onto it, or could undermine it. Use the BA20 or BA20A forms.
