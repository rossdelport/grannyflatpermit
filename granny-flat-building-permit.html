Question

Do I need a building permit for a granny flat?

Short answer

Yes. Since 2024 most granny flats up to 70 m² don't need planning approval, but every granny flat still needs a building permit and must meet the National Construction Code. Councils such as Wanneroo and Joondalup confirm this.

Last updated 12 November 2026

Yes, every granny flat needs a building permit

The 2024 changes made granny flats easier, but only on the planning side. The state's granny flat info sheet says you still need a building permit, and the granny flat must meet the National Construction Code. The City of Wanneroo and the City of Joondalup both say a building permit is always required.

Planning approval vs building permit

Planning approvalBuilding permit
Needed?Usually not, if it meets the deemed-to-comply rulesAlways
What it checksSize (70 m²), setbacks, site cover, height, privacyStructure, fire safety, energy, plumbing and the building code
Time limit60 days, or 90 with consultation10 business days (BA1) or 25 (BA2)

If your granny flat needs planning approval, it must come first. Wanneroo says the planning approval must be granted before the building permit.

BA1 or BA2?

  • BA2 (uncertified): the council checks the plans. Allowed for houses and Class 10 buildings, so a granny flat can use it. Decision within 25 business days.
  • BA1 (certified): a private building surveyor checks the plans first and issues a Certificate of Design Compliance. Decision within 10 business days.

Wanneroo has separate BA1 and BA2 checklists for residential buildings.

Who can build it

The state says there are no special exemptions for prefab or kit homes. A habitable building worth more than $20,000 that needs a permit must be built by a registered builder or an approved owner-builder. Electrical and plumbing work must be done by licensed trades.

State fees

A BA2 costs 0.32% of the value and a BA1 0.19% (plus the surveyor), at least $121. The Building Services Levy is 0.137% above $45,000, and the Construction Training Fund levy is 0.2% for work over $100,000. Use the fee calculator on GetMyPermit to work it out.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a building permit for a granny flat in WA?

Yes. The state's granny flat info sheet says a building permit is still needed, and councils such as Wanneroo and Joondalup say it's always required.

Do I need planning approval for a granny flat?

Usually not, if it's 70 m² or less and meets the R-Codes deemed-to-comply rules. If it doesn't, planning approval comes first.

Can I build a granny flat as an owner-builder?

Yes, if you're an approved owner-builder. Otherwise a habitable building over $20,000 must be built by a registered builder.

Does a kit or transportable granny flat need a permit?

Yes. The state says there are no specific exemptions for prefabricated buildings.
