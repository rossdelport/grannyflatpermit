City of Wanneroo granny flat rules

Short answer

In Wanneroo a granny flat always needs a building permit. It skips planning approval if it meets the R-Codes, including the 70 m² limit. On lots over 4,000 m², the City's policy lets it consider up to 100 m², through planning approval.

Last updated 11 November 2026

Approvals you need

The City of Wanneroo says a building permit is always required for a granny flat (ancillary dwelling). Planning approval may be needed too. If it is, it must be granted before the building permit is issued.

Under the state R-Codes, a granny flat on a residential lot doesn't need planning approval if it meets all the deemed-to-comply rules, including:

  • No more than 70 m² internal floor area
  • Behind the street setback line
  • Parking: one extra bay on most house lots, unless you're close to a frequent train or bus service
  • Leaves the house enough open space and outdoor living area
  • Meets the other R-Codes rules for single houses, such as setbacks and privacy

Wanneroo's own policy: bigger flats on big lots

Wanneroo has a Local Planning Policy for granny flats (LPP 2.6). Its main extra is size:

LotSize the City may consider
Most residential lotsUp to 70 m² (the R-Codes limit)
Lots over 4,000 m²Up to 100 m², if it stays subsidiary to the main house
Lots over 4,000 m² with special needsUp to 110 m² where residents have accessibility or medical needs and it's built to the Liveable Housing Design Guidelines "Silver Level"

Anything over 70 m² isn't deemed-to-comply, so it goes through planning approval. The policy says the larger size helps interpret the R-Codes' design principles; it doesn't change the 70 m² deemed-to-comply rule.

Rural and other non-R-Code land

Where the R-Codes don't apply, such as some rural zones, Wanneroo's policy says:

  • The granny flat must go with a single house that exists or is built at the same time
  • Only one granny flat per lot
  • Materials, colours and bulk shouldn't spoil the street or neighbours' outlook

What counts as a granny flat

The policy says a granny flat is self-contained: it should have a bathroom, toilet, kitchen and laundry. It can be detached, attached, or built into the house. Games rooms, sleep-outs and pool rooms that can't be lived in separately aren't granny flats.

How to apply

Wanneroo prefers online lodgement. Use its Certified (BA1) or Uncertified (BA2) residential building checklist, and its DA Checklist if you need planning approval. Already built without approval? The City has a separate Unauthorised Works checklist. Call 9405 5000 with questions.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need approval for a granny flat in Wanneroo?

A building permit is always required. Planning approval is only needed if the granny flat doesn't meet all the R-Codes deemed-to-comply rules, such as the 70 m² limit.

How big can a granny flat be in Wanneroo?

70 m² on most lots. On lots over 4,000 m², the City's policy lets it consider up to 100 m², or 110 m² for residents with accessibility or medical needs. Over 70 m² needs planning approval.

Can I have two granny flats in Wanneroo?

Usually no. Wanneroo's policy allows only one granny flat per lot where the R-Codes don't apply. Under the R-Codes, a granny flat goes with one house, and the higher-density rules allow one per site.

Does a Wanneroo granny flat need a laundry?

The City's policy says a self-contained granny flat should include a bathroom, toilet, kitchen and laundry.
