City of Cockburn granny flat rules

Short answer

In the City of Cockburn's Residential zone, a granny flat up to 70 m² doesn't need planning approval if it meets the R-Codes, sits behind the house's street setback and has one extra car bay (unless near frequent transport). Rural-type zones allow up to 100 m² but always need planning approval.

Last updated 24 November 2026

What counts as a granny flat

The City of Cockburn calls it an ancillary dwelling: a self-contained home on the same lot as another home. It can be a separate studio out the back, a studio above a garage, a self-contained wing or upstairs area in the house, or a dual-key layout.

Does it need planning approval?

ZonePlanning approval
ResidentialNot needed if it meets every rule below and the R-Codes
Resource, Rural Living and RuralAlways needed

Lots over 1,100 m² rated BAL-40 or Flame Zone also need planning approval, with a BAL assessment. After any planning approval, you still need a building permit.

Rules in the Residential zone

  • One granny flat per lot.
  • Up to 70 m² inside. The City will consider up to 100 m² if it's built to the R-Codes standards for aged and dependent housing (fully accessible), as a condition of planning approval.
  • Behind the street setback line of the main house.
  • The main house must still have its minimum open space and outdoor living area.
  • Must meet the other R-Codes rules: sun and airflow, height, setbacks, overlooking, overshadowing and landscaping.
  • One extra car bay, unless the lot is within 800 m of a train station on a frequent line or 250 m of a frequent bus route.

Rules in Resource, Rural Living and Rural zones

  • One per lot, generally up to 100 m² of living area (not counting verandahs, patios, alfresco or carports).
  • Usually behind the main building line.
  • Design, materials and colours should match or suit the house. No reflective or second-hand materials.

Call the City on 08 9411 3444. See granny flat parking, granny flats on rural lots and granny flats at the front.

Questions

Common questions

How big can a granny flat be in the City of Cockburn?

Up to 70 m² in the Residential zone, or up to 100 m² if it's built to accessible aged and dependent standards. In rural-type zones the usual limit is 100 m².

Do I need planning approval for a granny flat in Cockburn?

Not in the Residential zone if it meets all the R-Codes and City rules. It's always needed in the Resource, Rural Living and Rural zones.

Does a granny flat need a parking bay in Cockburn?

Yes, one extra bay, unless the lot is within 800 m of a frequent train station or 250 m of a frequent bus route.

Can a granny flat be in front of the house in Cockburn?

Not without planning approval. It needs to sit behind the street setback line of the main house.
