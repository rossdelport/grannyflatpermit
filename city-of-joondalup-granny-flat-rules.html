City of Joondalup granny flat rules

Short answer

In Joondalup, a granny flat usually skips planning approval if it's 70 m² or less, meets the R-Codes, matches the house in colour, roof pitch and materials, and has one extra car bay on most lower-density lots. It always needs a building permit.

Last updated 4 November 2026

When you don't need planning approval

The City of Joondalup says a granny flat is generally exempt from planning (development) approval if it meets the standard R-Codes rules, including:

  • Floor area no more than 70 m²
  • Parking (see below)
  • All other R-Codes rules, such as height and setbacks
  • Design that matches the house: the same colour, roof pitch and materials
  • Any extra rules if your lot is in a structure plan area

Check your lot's R-Code and whether it's in a structure plan area with the City's online mapping system.

Parking: Joondalup can ask for an extra bay

Main homeExtra car bay for the granny flat?
Single house, R40 or lowerYes, one bay, unless within 800 m walk of a train station or 250 m of a high-frequency bus route
Grouped dwelling, R25 or lowerYes, one bay, with the same exemptions
Single house R50 or higher, or grouped dwelling R30 or higherNo

Check your R-Code first, and leave room for the extra bay if you need one.

If you don't meet the rules

You'll need development approval: an application form, building plans and a cover letter, plus the application fee.

Building permit

Every granny flat in Joondalup needs a building permit. You can lodge:

  • a BA1 (certified), with a Certificate of Design Compliance (BA3) from a registered building surveyor, or
  • a BA2 (uncertified), with the City's checklist for dwellings and habitable additions.

Renting it out

The City confirms a granny flat can be rented out separately from the main house.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need planning approval for a granny flat in Joondalup?

Not usually. It's generally exempt if it's 70 m² or less, meets parking and all other R-Codes rules, and matches the house's colour, roof pitch and materials.

Does a granny flat in Joondalup need its own parking bay?

Yes, one extra bay if the main home is a single house on R40 or lower, or a grouped dwelling on R25 or lower, unless you're within 800 m of a train station or 250 m of a high-frequency bus route.

Do I need a building permit for a granny flat in Joondalup?

Yes, always. Lodge a BA1 with a Certificate of Design Compliance, or a BA2 with the City's checklist.

Can I rent out a granny flat in Joondalup?

Yes. The City says a granny flat can be rented out separately from the main house.
