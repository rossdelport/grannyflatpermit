How-to guide

How to turn an existing building into a granny flat

Short answer

To turn a shed, garage or carport into a granny flat in WA, you need a building permit before work starts. The building usually needs upgrades such as a 2.4 m lined ceiling, a damp-proof floor, windows of at least a tenth of the floor area and energy efficiency. Granny flats are usually capped at 70 m².

Last updated 26 November 2026

Why you need approval

A shed, garage, carport or patio is built as a non-habitable building. Living in it changes its use, so it has to be brought up to the standard for a home. Councils such as the City of Armadale say turning a structure into a room you live in always needs a building permit, and so does any granny flat. The permit comes before work starts.

Step 1: check it can be a granny flat

  • Size: usually up to 70 m², not counting open verandahs or carports. Try the size calculator.
  • How many: usually one granny flat per lot.
  • Planning: if it meets the R-Codes deemed-to-comply rules for granny flats, it usually doesn't need planning approval. If not, get planning approval first. See granny flat planning approval.
  • Parking: converting a carport only works if there's room elsewhere on the lot for a car.

Step 2: bring the building up to standard

The City of Kalamunda's checklist for turning a patio, shed, carport or garage into a habitable room is a good guide. The main points:

ItemWhat's usually needed
CeilingAt least 2.4 m high, lined, with a ceiling
FloorDamp-proofed. An old slab or paving may need replacing with a new slab and membrane
WallsBuilt to keep moisture out
LightWindows or glass doors at least one-tenth of the floor area
AirNatural ventilation
BedroomsHard-wired smoke alarms may be needed
EnergyProof it meets the building code's energy rules
Meter boxMoved to an outside wall if it's inside
No sewer?A health application for an effluent system

A structural engineer often has to check the existing frame and footings. Councils vary, so check your own council's list.

Step 3: apply

Lodge a BA1 (certified by a private building surveyor) or BA2 (checked by the council) with your plans and reports. Councils usually decide in 10 business days (BA1) or 25 (BA2). See the granny flat building permit.

Questions

Common questions

Can I turn my shed into a granny flat in WA?

Often yes, but you need a building permit first, and the shed usually needs work such as a 2.4 m lined ceiling, damp-proof floor, windows, ventilation and energy efficiency upgrades.

Can I convert my garage into a granny flat?

Usually, if it can meet the granny flat rules (often up to 70 m², one per lot) and the building standards for a home. A building permit is needed before work starts.

Can I turn my carport into a room?

The City of Kalamunda says a carport can be converted only if there's enough space elsewhere on the lot for a vehicle shelter.

What ceiling height does a converted granny flat need?

Usually at least 2.4 m for a habitable room, and the room must be lined with a ceiling.
