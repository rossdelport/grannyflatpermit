City of Canning granny flat rules

Short answer

In the City of Canning you can build one granny flat up to 70 m² per property, and rent it to anyone. Planning approval usually isn't needed if it meets the R-Codes, but a building permit always is. The City decides in 10 working days (BA1) or 25 (BA2).

Last updated 23 November 2026

The main rules

  • Size: up to 70 m², not counting open verandahs or carports. A bigger one needs a planning application.
  • How many: one granny flat per property.
  • Renting: allowed, including to people who aren't family.
  • Subdividing: building a granny flat doesn't let you split the lot, unless the planning scheme already allows it.
  • Attached or detached: it can be attached to, built into, or separate from the house.

Planning approval

For most homes you don't need planning approval if the granny flat meets every "deemed-to-comply" rule in the R-Codes. Some zones still need it. If you're unsure, the City offers a deemed-to-comply check before you lodge your building permit. To vary the rules, you apply for development approval with good reasons, and neighbours may be asked. Check your zone and density with Planning Services on (08) 9231 0606.

Questions

Common questions

How big can a granny flat be in the City of Canning?

Up to 70 m², not counting open verandahs or carports. Bigger ones need a planning application.

Can I rent out a granny flat in Canning?

Yes. The City says granny flats can be rented to people who aren't family.

Do I need planning approval for a granny flat in Canning?

Usually not, if it meets every R-Codes deemed-to-comply rule. Some zones still need it, and the City can check your plans first.

How long does Canning take to issue a granny flat permit?

10 working days for a certified (BA1) application and 25 working days for an uncertified (BA2) one.
