Town of Victoria Park granny flat rules

Short answer

In the Town of Victoria Park, a granny flat up to 70 m² doesn't need planning approval if it meets the R-Codes rules and the property isn't heritage-protected. It always needs a building permit. You may need one extra car bay, depending on how close you are to public transport.

Last updated 17 November 2026

Can you build one?

Yes. The Town of Victoria Park says you can build an ancillary dwelling (granny flat) with a single house, grouped dwelling or multiple dwelling, whatever the size of your land, as long as it meets the R-Codes rules for setbacks, site cover, height, privacy and access.

When you don't need planning approval

You don't need development approval if the granny flat meets all the deemed-to-comply rules and the property isn't heritage-protected. Every granny flat on a heritage-listed property, or in a heritage area, needs development approval.

The main rules for a granny flat with a house (R-Codes Volume 1, Part B):

  • Same lot as a single house or grouped dwelling.
  • No more than 70 m² inside (including built-in furniture, not counting a garage).
  • Behind the street setback line.
  • The main house still keeps its required open space and outdoor living area.
  • All other R-Codes rules (privacy, boundary walls, setbacks) still apply.

Not sure? The Town offers a paid deemed-to-comply check that tells you if you need a development application.

Parking

Where your lot isExtra car bay for the granny flat
Location A: within 800 m walk of a high-frequency train station, or 250 m of a high-frequency bus stopNone
Location B: everywhere elseOne extra bay on the lot

The main house must keep its own parking too. Developments under Part C of the R-Codes don't need an extra bay.

Other things to know

  • Who can live there: anyone. Family, friends or a tenant.
  • Neighbours: you don't need their approval to apply. The Town may consult them if you ask to vary the rules.
  • Timeframe: 60 days for a development application, or 90 if neighbours are consulted. Lodge online.
  • Building permit: always needed, lodged with the Town.
  • Rates: once it's finished, the Valuer-General reviews your property's rental value and the Town sends an updated rates notice.
  • Services: you may need new meters or sub-meters for power, gas or water.

Questions

Common questions

Can I build a granny flat in the Town of Victoria Park?

Yes, with a single house, grouped dwelling or multiple dwelling, on any lot size, if it meets the R-Codes rules.

Do I need planning approval for a granny flat in Victoria Park?

Not if it meets all the deemed-to-comply rules and the property isn't heritage-protected. Heritage properties always need development approval.

Does a Victoria Park granny flat need its own car bay?

Only in Location B, which is anywhere not close to a high-frequency train station or bus stop.

Who can live in a granny flat in Victoria Park?

Anyone: a relative, a friend or a tenant.
