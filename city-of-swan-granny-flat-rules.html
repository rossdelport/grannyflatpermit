City of Swan granny flat rules

Short answer

In the City of Swan, every granny flat needs a building permit. You'll also need planning approval if it's over 70 m², you can't add an extra parking bay (unless you're near high-frequency transport), or, per the City's questionnaire, the lot is under 450 m². On rural land it must be within 10 m of the house.

Last updated 18 November 2026

Building permit

Every granny flat in the City of Swan needs a building permit. You can lodge a BA1 (certified by a private building surveyor), which the City has 10 business days to decide, or a BA2 (uncertified), which it has 25 business days to decide. Applications are lodged online through the City's ePathway portal.

Do you need planning approval?

The City has a granny flat questionnaire to work this out. It flags that you'll need a planning application (development approval) if:

QuestionPlanning application needed if
Is there a house on the same lot?No (a granny flat must go with a single house)
Lot sizeLess than 450 m²
Granny flat size (plot ratio)More than 70 m²
ParkingNo extra bay, unless you're within 800 m of a high-frequency train station or 250 m of a high-frequency bus route

The questionnaire also asks how wastewater will be handled, which matters on lots without sewer. The state R-Codes allow a granny flat on any size lot if it meets the standard rules, so ask the City which applies to your lot. Councils vary.

Rural lots (Policy POL-C-066)

On rural-zoned land, the City's Ancillary Accommodation policy says a granny flat should:

  • Be no bigger than 70 m², not counting verandahs, patios, carports, garages and pergolas.
  • Have one extra car bay.
  • Be within 10 m of the main house.

The City may add a condition that only dependent family members live there, and a granny flat can't be used as a reason to subdivide.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need council approval for a granny flat in the City of Swan?

You always need a building permit. You may also need planning approval, for example if the granny flat is over 70 m² or you can't provide an extra parking bay.

How big can a granny flat be in the City of Swan?

Up to 70 m² without a planning application. On rural land, the 70 m² doesn't include verandahs, patios, carports, garages and pergolas.

How far from the house can a granny flat be on a rural lot in Swan?

Within 10 m of the main house, under the City's Ancillary Accommodation policy POL-C-066.

How long does the City of Swan take to approve a granny flat?

10 business days for a certified BA1 building permit, or 25 business days for an uncertified BA2. Planning approval, if needed, comes first.
