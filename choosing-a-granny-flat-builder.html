How to choose a granny flat builder in Perth

Short answer

Check the builder is registered, get a written contract, keep the deposit to 6.5% or less, and see the home indemnity insurance certificate before paying anything. Ask exactly which approvals the quote includes, as every granny flat needs a building permit.

Last updated 5 November 2026

Seven checks before you sign

  1. Are they a registered builder? A granny flat is a home (Class 1a), so building work worth $20,000 or more that needs a permit must be done by a registered builder. The $50,000 exemption since July 2026 only covers sheds, garages, carports and patios. Check the builder with the state's Find a registered builder search.
  2. Is there a written contract? For home building contracts between $7,500 and $500,000, the Home Building Contracts Act applies. The contract must be in writing and signed by both of you, and the builder must give you the "Notice for the homeowner" first.
  3. Is the deposit 6.5% or less? That's the legal maximum before work starts on these contracts.
  4. Do progress payments match the work? You should only pay for work done or materials delivered. Upfront payments beyond the deposit aren't allowed and can affect your insurance claim.
  5. Have you seen the home indemnity insurance certificate? It's required for permit work over $20,000. Check it on QBE's online register, and don't pay anything until it's in place. It covers loss of deposit (up to $40,000) and incomplete or defective work (up to $200,000) if the builder dies, disappears or goes broke.
  6. Who handles the approvals? Ask if the quote includes the plans, energy efficiency report, engineering, building permit and, if needed, planning approval. A granny flat always needs a building permit.
  7. Will the design pass? Make sure it's no more than 70 m² and meets your council's rules. Some councils, like Joondalup, also want an extra parking bay and matching materials.

Watch out for "plans first" agreements

A preparation of plans agreement (PPA), where you pay a builder to draw plans before the main contract, isn't part of the building contract, so it isn't covered by home indemnity insurance.

After it's built

In WA, builders must fix defective work for six years after practical completion. Complaints go to the Building Commissioner.

Questions

Common questions

Does a granny flat builder need to be registered in WA?

Yes, if the work needs a building permit and is worth $20,000 or more, which covers almost every granny flat. The $50,000 exemption only applies to sheds, garages, carports and patios.

What is the maximum deposit for a granny flat in WA?

6.5% of the contract price, for home building contracts between $7,500 and $500,000.

Do I need home indemnity insurance for a granny flat?

Yes, for permit work over $20,000. Your builder must give you the certificate, and you can check it on QBE's online register.

How long is a builder responsible for defects in WA?

Six years from practical completion.
