City of Melville granny flat rules

Short answer

In Melville, a granny flat usually needs no planning approval in a residential zone if the lot is at least 450 m², it's up to 70 m², and there's an extra parking bay (unless you're near high-frequency transport). It always needs a building permit. You can rent it out, but not subdivide it.

Last updated 20 November 2026

When you don't need planning approval

In a residential zone, the City of Melville says you usually don't need planning approval if:

RuleLimit
Goes withA single house on the same lot
Lot sizeAt least 450 m²
SizeUp to 70 m² plot ratio area
ParkingOne extra bay, unless within 800 m of a train station or 250 m of a high-frequency bus route
DesignMeets all the R-Codes and local planning policies

Note the 450 m² lot size: on a smaller lot, check with the City first. In any zone other than residential, you always need planning approval. Councils vary.

Granny flats are only allowed where the title has no common property, so green title or survey strata only. You can't subdivide it off, but you can rent it to anyone, not just family.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need council approval for a granny flat in the City of Melville?

You always need a building permit. You usually don't need planning approval in a residential zone if the lot is at least 450 m², the granny flat is up to 70 m², and you add a parking bay.

Can I rent out a granny flat in Melville?

Yes. The City says it can be rented to people who aren't related to the people in the main house. It can't be subdivided off.

How much is a building permit for a granny flat in Melville?

$166.65 under $20,000. Over that, 0.19% of the contract price plus the 0.137% Building Services Levy, plus a $1,900 verge bond and $100 inspection fee.

Can I build a granny flat on a strata lot in Melville?

Only if the title has no common property, such as survey strata without common property.
