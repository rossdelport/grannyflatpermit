Question answer

Can you rent out a granny flat in WA?

Short answer

Yes. Since the April 2024 R-Codes changes, anyone can live in a WA granny flat, including tenants under a rental agreement. It must still be an approved dwelling, and it can't be sold separately from the main house.

Last updated 9 November 2026

Yes, since 2024

The state's granny flat changes to the R-Codes took effect in April 2024. The WA planning department's information sheet says anyone can live in a granny flat: "from a relative, friend or someone unknown to you who you or a real-estate agent enters into a rental agreement with."

The City of Joondalup says the same: a granny flat can be rented out separately from the main house, as long as it meets the R-Codes definition of a dwelling.

What changed

SizeVaried (some schemes 60 m²)Up to 70 m² without planning approval

Some council documents online still show the old rules. Check you're reading a current version.

What doesn't change

  • It stays on your title. A granny flat can't be subdivided or strata titled separately, so you can't sell it on its own. See subdividing a granny flat.
  • It must be a proper dwelling. It's a self-contained home, so a kitchen and bathroom are generally expected.
  • It needs a building permit and must meet the National Construction Code.
  • Planning rules still apply. Size, setbacks, open space and, in some councils, parking and matching the main house.

Before you rent it out

  • Check it was approved. An unapproved granny flat, or a shed or garage converted without approval, isn't a legal dwelling. Fix that first with a building approval certificate.
  • Normal renting rules apply. Once you sign a rental agreement, you're a landlord under WA tenancy law. Consumer Protection has guides for landlords.
  • Talk to your insurer and accountant. Renting can change your insurance and tax.

Questions

Common questions

Can I rent out my granny flat in WA?

Yes. Since the April 2024 R-Codes changes, anyone can live in a granny flat, including tenants under a rental agreement. It must meet the R-Codes definition of a dwelling.

Does a granny flat tenant have to be family in WA?

No. The state's information sheet says anyone can live in a granny flat, from a relative or friend to a tenant you don't know.

Can I sell my granny flat separately?

No. A granny flat can't be subdivided or strata titled separately from the main house.

Can I rent out a granny flat on a strata lot?

The R-Codes now allow granny flats on grouped dwelling and strata lots at all density codes in Volume 1. You may also need strata approval.

Do I need approval to rent out an existing granny flat?

Not to rent it, but it must have been approved as a dwelling. An unapproved granny flat needs to be made legal first, usually with a building approval certificate.
