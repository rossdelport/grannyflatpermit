Short answer

Every granny flat in WA needs a building permit. Since 10 April 2024 most don't need planning approval if they are 70 m² or smaller, on a residential lot, and meet the R-Codes' standard rules such as setbacks.

  • No minimum lot size on residential lots any more.
  • Anyone can live in it, and it can be rented out.
  • Rural lots often still need a development application.

What a granny flat application needs

More than a shed, less than a house.

  • Site plan with setbacks, open space and the main house.
  • Floor plans, elevations and sections of the granny flat.
  • Engineering for the slab or footings and the frame.
  • Energy efficiency assessment to the building code.
  • Bushfire assessment if the lot is in a bushfire-prone area.
  • Forms, owner consent and fees, then lodgement.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need council approval for a granny flat in WA?

You always need a building permit. Since 10 April 2024 you usually don't need planning approval for a granny flat up to 70 m² on a residential lot, as long as it meets the R-Codes' standard rules, including setbacks.

Is there a minimum lot size for a granny flat in WA?

Not any more on residential lots. The old 350 m² minimum lot size was removed in April 2024, so a compliant granny flat can go on a residential lot of any size if it fits within the setbacks and open space rules.

Can I rent out my granny flat?

Yes. In WA anyone can live in a granny flat, not just family, and it can be rented out separately from the main house.

How big can a granny flat be in WA?

Up to 70 m² of internal floor area under the R-Codes' ancillary dwelling rules. Bigger than that needs planning approval and may be assessed as a second house.

Does a granny flat need its own parking?

The R-Codes set parking rules for granny flats, and the 2024 changes made them more flexible, particularly near public transport. Your council's local policy can add to them. We check this as part of the application.

Who can lodge the building permit?

Your builder often does it as part of the build. If you are using a kit, a modular supplier or an owner-builder, we can prepare and lodge it for you.
