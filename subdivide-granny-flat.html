Question answer

Can I subdivide my granny flat later?

Short answer

Not as a granny flat. The R-Codes say a granny flat must stay on the same lot as the house, so strata or green-title subdivision isn't allowed. You can only subdivide if the lot meets the minimum site area for its R-Code, and the two homes become grouped dwellings or single houses.

Last updated 3 November 2026

Why you can't just split it off

Under the R-Codes, a granny flat (an "ancillary dwelling") has to be on the same lot as the main house. The state's R-Codes guidance says subdivision into strata, built-strata or green-title lots to put the granny flat on its own lot isn't permitted. The house and granny flat are treated as two dwellings on one lot.

The only way to subdivide

You can only subdivide if the lot meets the minimum site area for its R-Code, and the result meets all the other R-Codes rules. The two homes are then no longer a house and a granny flat. They become two grouped dwellings or two single houses.

That usually means more than just a title change. Each home has to meet the rules for a full dwelling, which can include things like open space, outdoor living areas and parking.

What this means when you build

  • A granny flat is limited to 70 m² and doesn't need a minimum lot size, which makes it easy to approve, but it stays tied to the house.
  • If you think you'll want to sell the second home separately one day, check your lot's R-Code and minimum site area first. Building to grouped dwelling rules from the start may make more sense.
  • Anyone can live in a granny flat now, including tenants, so renting it out doesn't need subdivision.

Sources

  • R-Codes Volume 1 Explanatory Guidelines, 2024 (WA Planning Commission)
  • Granny flats information sheet (Department of Planning, Lands and Heritage)

Questions

Common questions

Can I subdivide my granny flat in WA?

Not as a granny flat. The R-Codes don't allow a granny flat to be put on its own lot. You can only subdivide if the lot meets the minimum site area for its R-Code, and the two homes then become grouped dwellings or single houses.

Can I sell my granny flat separately?

Not while it's a granny flat, because it must stay on the same lot as the main house. Selling separately needs a subdivision that meets the R-Codes rules for two full dwellings.

Can I strata title a granny flat?

No. The R-Codes guidance says strata, built-strata and green-title subdivision of a granny flat isn't permitted, unless the site meets the requirements for two grouped dwellings or single houses.

Can I rent out my granny flat without subdividing?

Yes. There's no longer a rule that only family can live in a granny flat, so you can rent it out on the same lot.
