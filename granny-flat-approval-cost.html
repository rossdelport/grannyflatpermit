Cost guide

How much does granny flat approval cost?

Short answer

For a $150,000 granny flat that doesn't need planning approval, the state fees come to about $985: the 0.32% BA2 permit fee, the 0.137% Building Services Levy and the 0.2% Construction Training Fund levy. Plans, engineering and any planning approval cost extra.

Last updated 14 November 2026

If you also need planning approval, add up to 0.382% of the cost, for example up to $573 on a $150,000 granny flat (City of Joondalup's fee schedule).

Do you need planning approval?

Usually not. Most granny flats up to 70 m² that meet the R-Codes' deemed-to-comply rules skip planning approval, so you only pay the building permit fees. Planning approval is needed if the granny flat breaks a rule, such as a setback, or the land isn't zoned residential.

Other costs to budget for

  • Plans and engineering for the application
  • Verge bond, which some councils charge on bigger jobs (for example $1,900 plus a $100 inspection fee at Melville, for work over $20,000)
  • Home indemnity insurance, which a registered builder usually arranges for home building work over $20,000
  • Retrospective approval if the granny flat is already built: Joondalup adds a penalty of twice the planning fee

Questions

Common questions

How much does granny flat approval cost in WA?

For a $150,000 granny flat with no planning approval, about $985 in state fees: $480 BA2 fee, $205.50 Building Services Levy and $300 Construction Training Fund levy.

Do I need to pay for planning approval for a granny flat?

Usually not. Most granny flats up to 70 m² that meet the R-Codes don't need it. If yours does, the fee is up to 0.382% of the cost for work from $50,000 to $500,000.

Is a BA1 or BA2 cheaper for a granny flat?

The council fee for a BA1 is lower (0.19% vs 0.32%), but you also pay a private building surveyor.

What is the Construction Training Fund levy?

A 0.2% levy on building work over $100,000. Since 1 July 2026 it's paid directly to the fund, and councils ask for the receipt.
