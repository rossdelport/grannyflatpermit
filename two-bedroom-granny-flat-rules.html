Question answer

Can a granny flat have 2 bedrooms in WA?

Short answer

Yes. WA's rules limit a granny flat's size, not its bedrooms. To skip planning approval it must be 70 m² or less inside the walls, and the state's guidelines say that's enough for a single or two-bedroom flat. You still need a building permit.

Last updated 7 November 2026

The rule is about size, not bedrooms

WA's R-Codes don't set a maximum number of bedrooms for a granny flat (the official name is "ancillary dwelling"). What they limit is the size: 70 m² of internal floor area if you want to skip planning approval. The state's own explanatory guidelines say 70 m² "allows for a single or two-bedroom dwelling".

Fitting two bedrooms into 70 m²

The 70 m² is measured from the inside face of the walls, so the wall thickness doesn't count against you. Even so, two bedrooms, a bathroom, a kitchen and a living area is a tight fit. Good designs usually:

  • combine the kitchen, living and dining into one open space
  • keep hallways short or skip them
  • put the laundry inside the bathroom
  • use built-in robes instead of walk-in ones

Check your design with the granny flat 70 m² calculator.

What else the rules expect

  • A kitchen and bathroom. A granny flat is self-contained, so a separate kitchen and bathroom are generally expected. The guidelines say a laundry isn't essential for planning.
  • Setbacks, site cover, height and privacy. It must meet the deemed-to-comply rules for your lot, as well as the size limit.
  • Parking. On most house lots, one extra parking bay is needed unless you're within an 800 m walk of a train station or 250 m of a high-frequency bus stop.
  • No separate title. A granny flat can't be subdivided off as its own lot.

Going over 70 m²

A bigger flat isn't automatically refused, but it no longer meets the deemed-to-comply rules. That means you need planning approval, and councils vary in what they'll accept.

A building permit is still needed

Whatever the number of bedrooms, the state is clear: you still need a building permit from your council, and the flat must meet the National Construction Code. See the full granny flat approval process or the granny flat rules in WA.

Questions

Common questions

Can a granny flat have 2 bedrooms in WA?

Yes. The R-Codes limit the size, not the bedrooms. WA's explanatory guidelines say the 70 m² limit allows for a single or two-bedroom dwelling.

Can a granny flat have 3 bedrooms in WA?

There's no bedroom limit, but fitting three bedrooms plus a kitchen, bathroom and living area into 70 m² is very hard. A bigger flat needs planning approval.

How is the 70 m² granny flat size measured?

As internal floor area, measured from the finished inside surface of the walls.

Does a two-bedroom granny flat need extra parking?

On most house lots, yes: one extra bay, unless you're close to a train station or high-frequency bus stop. Higher-density lots don't need one.

Does a two-bedroom granny flat need a building permit?

Yes. Every granny flat needs a building permit and must meet the National Construction Code, even when planning approval isn't needed.
