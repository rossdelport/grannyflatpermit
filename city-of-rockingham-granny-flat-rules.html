City of Rockingham granny flat rules

Short answer

In Rockingham, a granny flat can be up to 70 m² on any lot size, one per lot, and can be rented out. It usually doesn't need planning approval if it meets the standard R-Codes rules, but it always needs a building permit.

Last updated 15 November 2026

The basics

In the R-Codes, a granny flat is called an ancillary dwelling. The City of Rockingham sums up the key rules:

  • Up to 70 m² internal floor area.
  • Only one per lot.
  • Allowed on any lot size: there's no minimum.
  • Can be lived in by family, friends or tenants.
  • May need one extra parking bay, depending on location and zoning.
  • Must meet the National Construction Code.

Planning approval

The City says single houses on residential land don't need development approval. A granny flat usually doesn't either, if it meets all the deemed-to-comply rules of the R-Codes (size, setbacks, site cover, height, privacy and parking).

You will need approval if:

  • it doesn't meet one of the standard R-Codes rules, or
  • the lot is zoned Special Residential or Special Rural, where the City needs town planning consent for any development, or
  • the site is in a heritage-listed area.

Extra rules in the city centre

Inside the Rockingham Strategic Centre (the city centre plan area), a granny flat must also:

  • sit behind the main house or be an extension of it, and
  • use materials and colours that suit the main house.

The plan restricts sheds, dongas and sea containers that haven't been properly converted.

Building permit: always

The City is clear: a building permit is needed in all cases before work starts. There are no exemptions for a granny flat.

Questions

Common questions

Can I build a granny flat in Rockingham?

Yes, on any residential lot size. It can be up to 70 m², one per lot, and needs a building permit.

Do I need planning approval for a granny flat in Rockingham?

Not usually, if it meets all the deemed-to-comply R-Codes rules. Special Residential and Special Rural lots, heritage areas and designs that don't comply need approval.

Can I rent out a granny flat in Rockingham?

Yes. The City says a granny flat may be lived in by family, friends or tenants.

How much are council fees for a granny flat in Rockingham?

For a $150,000 granny flat with a BA1: about $285 permit fee, $205.50 levy and a $335 kerb inspection fee, plus the certificate of design compliance.
