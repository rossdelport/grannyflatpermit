Question answer

Is there a minimum lot size for a granny flat?

Short answer

No. The old 350 m² minimum was removed in the R-Codes Volume 1 (2024), from mid-April 2024. A granny flat of up to 70 m² can go on any size lot, including strata lots, as long as it meets the standard rules for setbacks, open space, height, privacy and access.

The changes took effect in mid-April 2024. Some websites still quote 350 m² or 450 m² minimums. Those are out of date.

So can any lot have a granny flat?

Any size lot can, if the granny flat fits the standard rules (the "deemed-to-comply" rules). Those include:

  • Setbacks from the boundaries, including your scheme's setbacks.
  • Site cover: the lot must keep its required open space with the granny flat added.
  • Height.
  • Visual privacy towards the neighbours.
  • Access to the granny flat.

On a small lot, open space and setbacks are usually what stop a granny flat, not a minimum lot size. If yours can't meet one of the rules, it isn't refused automatically; it just needs planning approval, where council assesses it on its merits.

Parking

On most house lots the R-Codes ask for one extra parking bay for a granny flat, unless you're within an 800 m walk of a train station or 250 m of a high-frequency bus stop. Higher-density lots don't need one. See granny flat parking.

A building permit is always needed

Skipping planning approval doesn't mean skipping the building permit. Every granny flat needs one and must meet the National Construction Code.

Questions

Common questions

What is the minimum lot size for a granny flat in WA?

There isn't one any more. The 350 m² minimum was removed in the R-Codes Volume 1 (2024), effective mid-April 2024. A granny flat of up to 70 m² can go on any size lot if it meets the deemed-to-comply rules.

Can I build a granny flat on a 300 m² block in Perth?

Possibly. There's no minimum lot size, but it must meet the setbacks, open space, height, privacy and access rules. On small lots, open space is often the limit. If it can't meet them, it needs planning approval.

Can I build a granny flat on a strata lot?

Since 2024 the R-Codes allow granny flats on grouped dwelling and strata lots at all density codes in Volume 1, subject to the same deemed-to-comply rules.

Does a granny flat need its own parking bay in WA?

On most house lots, yes: one extra bay, unless you're close to a train station or high-frequency bus stop. Higher-density lots don't need one.
