Put in your own numbers. Nothing is sent anywhere. This is before tax.

Can you rent out a granny flat in WA?

Yes. Since the R-Codes changed in 2024, a granny flat (ancillary dwelling) can be lived in by family, friends or tenants. It no longer has to be for a family member. Read the full rules in can you rent out a granny flat in WA?

How the calculator works

  • Rent in = weekly rent × weeks rented. Allow a week or two a year for gaps between tenants.
  • Net = rent in minus your yearly costs. Include insurance, repairs, any extra council or water charges, and bills you cover for the tenant.
  • Return = net ÷ build cost. It's a simple way to compare the granny flat with other uses of the money.
  • Years to cover the build = build cost ÷ net.

The numbers in the boxes are only examples. We haven't found an official source for typical Perth granny flat rents, so check rents for similar places near you before you rely on the result.

Tax

The ATO says you must keep records of rental income and declare it in your tax return. Some expenses may be claimable. If the granny flat is on the same title as your home, renting it out means you're not entitled to the full main residence exemption from capital gains tax when you sell. Get advice from your accountant before you start.

Approvals first

A granny flat needs a building permit, and planning approval if it doesn't meet the standard R-Codes rules. See granny flat approval cost and granny flat cost in Perth for the build side of the sum.

Questions

Common questions

Can I rent out my granny flat to anyone in WA?

Yes. Since 2024, the R-Codes let a granny flat be lived in by family, friends or tenants.

Is granny flat rent taxable?

Yes. The ATO says you must keep records of rental income and declare it in your tax return. Some expenses may be claimable.

Does renting a granny flat affect capital gains tax?

The ATO says you're not entitled to the full main residence exemption if you rent out part of your home. Ask your accountant how it applies to you.

How do I work out if a granny flat is worth it?

Take the yearly rent, subtract your yearly costs, and divide by the build cost to get a simple return. The calculator above does this for you.
